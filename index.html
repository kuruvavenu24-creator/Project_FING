About This App

This app combines a calm sky blue interface with a live front-camera view, hand landmark detection, and a soft animated cloud background.

When you start the camera, the app looks for your hands, draws finger and palm landmarks in real time, and tracks the index fingertip as an on-screen mouse pointer.

Run it on localhost for camera access, then raise one hand and point with your index finger to explore the interactive camera experience.
